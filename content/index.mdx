# Lee Beydoun

I'm revisiting the foundations of mathematics and writing down the explanations I wish I had met the
first time. The result is a guide that starts with basic proofs and builds towards geometry,
functions, trigonometry, complex numbers and determinants.

[Start with Basic proofs](./maths/basic-proofs/introduction.mdx), or use the chapter list to jump to
the part you want to study.
