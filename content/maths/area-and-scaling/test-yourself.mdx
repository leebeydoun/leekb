These questions check the ideas behind the chapter rather than long calculation: why lengths grow by
$$r$$, areas by $$r^2$$ and volumes by $$r^3$$, how the grid squeeze pins down an area, which steps
are taken from the picture, and why the same $$\pi$$ measures both a disc and its circle. Pizzas,
maps, giants and crushed ice all make an appearance. Each answer comes with an explanation that
links back to the page it is about. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can stop and come
back later.
