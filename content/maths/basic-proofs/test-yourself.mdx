title: Concept check
description: A diagnostic multiple-choice quiz on the ideas in the Basic proofs chapter.
---

These questions diagnose whether you recognise the ideas behind the chapter. Each answer comes with
an explanation and a link back to the relevant guide, and progress is saved in this browser.

A good score shows conceptual recognition, not yet the ability to construct a proof. After the quiz,
use the [chapter exercises](./exercises.mdx) to practise translating, repairing and writing
arguments without answer choices.
