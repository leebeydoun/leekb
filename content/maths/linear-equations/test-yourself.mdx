These questions diagnose whether you recognise the chapter's ideas: what the definitions say, why
each rule holds and which common transformations fail. Every answer includes an explanation and a
link to the relevant guide, and progress is saved in this browser.

A strong score shows conceptual recognition, not yet independent solving or modelling. Use the
[chapter exercises](./exercises.mdx) to demonstrate that you can choose a method, preserve the
solution set, solve, check and interpret an answer without options to choose from.
